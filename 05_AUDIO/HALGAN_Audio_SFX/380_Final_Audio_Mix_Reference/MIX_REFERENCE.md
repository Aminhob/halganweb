# Final audio mix reference

Suggested starting fader levels relative to full scale (adjust by listening on a phone speaker):

| Bus | Starting level |
|---|---:|
| Punch impacts | -6 dB |
| UI | -12 dB |
| Crowd ambience | -24 dB |
| Music | -18 dB |
| Voice chat | -9 dB |

Duck music 3-6 dB during heavy impacts and voice chat. Keep voice chat intelligible above ambience, and check the final master for clipping and device loudness. These are starting points, not measured loudness targets.
