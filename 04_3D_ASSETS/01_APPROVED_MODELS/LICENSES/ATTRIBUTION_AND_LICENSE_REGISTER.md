This permits commercial sharing/adaptation subject to creator credit, license link and change notices. They were not downloaded or packaged: the API and browser download require authentication. No attribution obligation arises from an unused candidate. Metadata evidence is under _REPORTS/BATCH_19. No quarantined or unclear-license mesh was acquired.
